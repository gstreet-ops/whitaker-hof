# 15 of 515 - The Lou Whitaker Hall of Fame Case

A single-page argument site. Lou Whitaker finished with 75.1 WAR, more than
any second baseman elected to Cooperstown in the last forty years outside the
pre-war giants - and lasted one year on the BBWAA ballot, named on 15 of 515
ballots in 2001.

## Running it

No build step, no dependencies. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

## Sources

- Career statistics, WAR, WAR7 and JAWS: Baseball-Reference
- Voting records and Era Committee results: National Baseball Hall of Fame

Statistics current as of August 2026, following the Class of 2026 elections.
